Report counts and uncertainty, not only percentages. Segment complexity and unresolved cases. Distinguish a forecast, pilot observation, and realized operating result. A zero denominator means the rate is undefined, not zero.

## Decision

Continue, improve, expand, consolidate, or retire. Record evidence, owner, actions, deadline, and next review. Link the decision to the future process and user experience, not just the model configuration.
