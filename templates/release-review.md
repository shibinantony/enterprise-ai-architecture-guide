Report counts, denominators, uncertainty, and uncovered cases. Do not turn an unexecuted test into a pass. The local policy simulator does not supply production acceptance evidence for these controls.

## Release decision

Record business and user readiness, approved scope, unresolved issues, accepted residual risks, accepting authorities, validity period, value and service thresholds, rollout/rollback criteria, and next review. Each owner accepts their actual responsibilities; an approval is an informed decision, not an agent-added checkbox.
