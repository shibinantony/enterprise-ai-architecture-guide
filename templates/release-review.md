Report counts, denominators, uncertainty, and uncovered cases. Do not turn an unexecuted test into a pass. The local policy simulator does not supply production acceptance evidence for these controls.

## Release decision

Record approved scope, unresolved issues, accepted residual risks, accepting authorities, validity period, monitoring thresholds, rollback/revocation criteria, and next review trigger. A human approval must represent an informed decision under actual authority, not a checkbox added by the agent.
