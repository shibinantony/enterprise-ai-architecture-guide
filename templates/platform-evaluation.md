## Price and outcome record

Record model/SKU, runtime mode, region/global scope, currency, date, units, discounts, minima, exclusions, tokens/calls, runtime metering assumptions, human effort, and verified outcomes. Identify every synthetic estimate and every quote still required.

## Decision

State selected approach and configuration, reasons, alternatives, gate results, sensitivity, dependencies, residual risks, owner, review date, and reconsideration triggers. A public documentation comparison is evidence for a trial plan, not a completed enterprise assessment.
